## What it does

- **Audio Unit inserts on system audio.** Up to five effects on whatever your Mac is playing — a DAW, a browser tab, Apple Music. Drag to reorder, one button bypasses the whole chain.
- **The meter reads the output of the chain.** Momentary, short-term and integrated LUFS plus True Peak, computed with libebur128 to EBU R128 / ITU-R BS.1770-4. Bypass the inserts and watch what they cost you.
- **A room correction curve on everything you listen to.** Including the reference you pulled up in a browser, not only what is inside the session.
- **Output switching that a DAW follows.** Change the Mac's output device mid-playback without opening the playback engine settings or reopening the session.
- **The reading lives in the notch**, or in a floating window on Macs without one. Targets for Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube and R128, plus a saved reference and the distance from it.
